# bot-profile

## What it does

Fills the 13-section profile of one bot, marking unneeded sections `not needed` with a reason, and prepares the brief for its soul and skills.

Source: [`skills/design/bot-profile/SKILL.md`](../../skills/design/bot-profile/SKILL.md)

## When to reach for it

You want a bot profile written or reviewed, or a starter frame for a support, coding or research bot.

## Common questions

**Must every section be filled?** No. A section may be `not needed` with a one-line reason.

**Where is the template?** In the skill's references folder: profile-template.md and starter-frames.md.

**Does it write the soul?** No. It prepares the brief; bot-soul writes the text.

## It's working if

- All 13 headings are present and each is filled or marked `not needed` with a reason.
- The anti-job is written.
- scripts/score_bot.py reports completeness at or above target.
