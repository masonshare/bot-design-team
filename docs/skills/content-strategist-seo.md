# content-strategist-seo

## What it does

Acts as an SEO editorial director: plans topic clusters and pillar pages, classifies search intent from the live results page, writes content briefs tight enough for a writer to follow, sets experience, expertise, authority and trust signals, judges programmatic SEO, audits and refreshes existing content, plans internal links, and sets measurement. It hands the actual writing to a copywriting skill. Body in English, description bilingual.

SEO writing usually breaks at planning: wrong intent, two pages competing for one query, a vague brief, invented numbers. The skill always delivers one brief (for a pillar page, with blanks for data you must supply) so the plan is concrete. Examples are invented (a home composting topic). Reference points: 5 to 10 cluster pages per pillar; each cluster page links up to the pillar and to 2 to 3 sibling pages; refresh candidates sit at positions about 4 to 20 with steady impressions; re-measure at 30, 90 and 180 days; mixed-intent score 0 to 3 per type. Every number in this skill is a labelled reference point, not a fact about your market or channel; the skill asks for your own figures and does not use Vietnamese market data.

## When to reach for it

Someone asks for a content strategy, a topic cluster, a pillar page, a content brief, a search-intent check, a refresh of old content, internal links, or whether programmatic SEO is worth it. It manages a separate copywriting skill for the writing and keeps the phrase "if installed, otherwise do it in place" for other skills. For ad copy or landing pages use `copywriter-skill`.

## Common questions

**Does it browse the live results page?** It asks for a search tool if the platform has one; if not, it asks you to paste what the results page shows.

**Will it give me numbers like traffic forecasts?** No. Where data is needed the brief has a blank for you to fill, never an invented figure.

**What is the minimum I get?** One finished brief for a pillar page, plus the cluster plan and intent classification.

## It's working if

- Each target query has an intent label based on the results page, not on the keyword alone.
- At least one complete content brief is delivered, with blanks for data you must supply.
- Internal links and a re-measure schedule (30, 90, 180 days) are stated.
