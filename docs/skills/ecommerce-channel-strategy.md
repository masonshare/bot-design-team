# ecommerce-channel-strategy

## What it does

Chooses and expands sales channels for a small online shop by business stage, then ends with a channel decision matrix and next actions. It asks five facts first, covers eight common channel types, and has a separate section on price conflict between channels.

Small shops tend to pick channels by feel or open too many at once. Resources, not marketplaces, are the real limit, so the skill picks by stage: one or two channels in stage one, a second only when the first is steady. Reference points used: own website only when gross margin is roughly above 50 percent; about 50 orders a day on channel one before opening channel two (a starting assumption to replace with real numbers); warn partners about 3 days before a big price cut. Every number in this skill is a labelled reference point, not a fact about your market; the skill asks for your own figures. Trade-off: it may advise one big marketplace first even where another channel suits the product better.

## When to reach for it

An owner asks which marketplace to sell on, marketplace versus social commerce versus own website, when to open another channel, or how to avoid price chaos between channels. For sales numbers use `ecommerce-kpi-analytics`; for the product range use `ecommerce-product-strategy`.

## Common questions

**Does it name one best marketplace?** It names a priority channel for your stage after five questions, and shows the matrix so you can see the trade-offs.

**Does it work if the market changes?** Platform names are examples only. Revisit it when a significant new channel appears or a platform changes its fee model.

**Does it open accounts or change prices?** No. It only recommends.

## It's working if

- It asks its five questions before recommending anything.
- The answer ends with a decision matrix and a short list of next actions.
- Any second-channel advice says how to keep prices consistent across channels.
