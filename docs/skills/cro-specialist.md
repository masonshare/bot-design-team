# cro-specialist

## What it does

Conversion rate optimisation: diagnoses why a page, form or flow does not convert using behavioural and heuristic frameworks (LIFT, conversion trinity, B=MAP, research-first process), prescribes evidence-based fixes, and designs A/B tests with hypothesis, metric, sample size, duration and stop rules. Body in English, description bilingual.

When a page does not convert, people guess and run random A/B tests. The skill forces an order: diagnose, prescribe, test. Each finding links to a framework, each fix to a hypothesis with a "because", each test to a metric, sample size and duration. It first rules out trivial causes, and it includes stop rules and how to read results. The sample size formula is a quick approximation the skill adds itself; check it with a calculator. Reference points: about 500 conversions a month to split test (below that, make the change and measure before and after); 95 percent confidence and 80 percent power; per-variant sample of about 16 x p x (1 - p) divided by the squared difference; run whole weeks, at least one business cycle; rethink a test over about 6 to 8 weeks; PIE and ICE scored 1 to 10. Every number in this skill is a labelled reference point, not a fact about your market or channel; the skill asks for your own figures and does not use Vietnamese market data.

## When to reach for it

Someone asks why people are not converting, to audit a landing page, to fix checkout drop-off or form abandonment, or to design an A/B test. It can hand a plain-text brief to a designer or developer. For page structure checks on a marketplace store use `ecommerce-store-optimization`; for the whole-journey view use `journey-mapper`; for the copy itself use `copywriter-skill`.

## Common questions

**What if I have too few conversions to test?** It says so and recommends making the change and measuring before and after instead of a split test.

**Is the sample size formula exact?** No, it is a planning approximation. Verify it with a sample size calculator.

**Who does the fixes?** It writes a handoff block for a designer or developer; it does not change your site.

## It's working if

- Each finding names a framework, and each fix has a hypothesis with a "because" clause.
- Each test has a primary metric, a guardrail, a sample size, a duration in whole weeks and a stop rule.
- Low-traffic cases are told to measure before and after instead of splitting.
