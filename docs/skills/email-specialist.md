# email-specialist

## What it does

Designs email programmes as a direct-response strategist: journeys (welcome, nurture, cart recovery, win-back, post-purchase), lifecycle logic, list health and deliverability basics, newsletter plans, subject lines and preview text, ethical use of persuasion triggers, a testing plan, and a per-email brief for the copywriting skill. It drafts only and never sends or connects to a sending tool. Body in English, description bilingual.

Email is an active channel with a high return but is often done as scattered single sends, with no exit conditions, no routing after a sequence, and no one diagnosing list decay. It always gives 2 to 3 subject lines per email with preview text and a recommended pick. Consent and legal rules are part of the skill. Intensity is gentle, balanced or assertive. Reference points: at most 3 triggers per email with 1 main trigger; welcome of 4 to 6 emails over 7 to 10 days; cart recovery of 3 emails at about 1 hour, 24 hours and 48 to 72 hours; win-back of 2 to 4 emails over 2 to 3 weeks, then stop; about 3 to 4 useful emails per promotional one; hard bounces under 1 percent, spam complaints under 0.1 percent, unsubscribes under 0.5 percent per send; the first 40 to 50 characters of preview text matter most. Every number in this skill is a labelled reference point, not a fact about your market or channel; the skill asks for your own figures and does not use Vietnamese market data. ## When to reach for it

Someone asks to design a welcome sequence, abandoned cart emails, a win-back, a newsletter plan, subject lines, or an email test plan, or to diagnose a declining list. It cannot send anything. For the copy itself it briefs `copywriter-skill`; related soft links are `brand-architect`, `journey-mapper`, `ecommerce-promotion-design` and `ecommerce-kpi-analytics`.

## Common questions

**Will it send my emails?** No. It drafts and plans; sending is done in your own email tool.

**Does it cover consent and legal basics?** Yes, a consent and rules section is part of the skill. It does not replace legal advice.

**Why 2 to 3 subject lines?** So you have something to test and a recommended pick with the reason.

## It's working if

- Each journey has an exit condition and a routing step for what happens after the sequence.
- Each email has 2 to 3 subject lines with preview text and a recommended one.
- The plan includes a list-health check and a testing plan, and nothing is sent.
