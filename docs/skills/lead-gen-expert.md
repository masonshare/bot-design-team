# lead-gen-expert

## What it does

Designs and drafts lead magnets (opt-ins) that solve one specific, urgent problem. It works in three roles: offer architect (pick the concept and format), funnel architect (place it in a funnel with a follow-up path) and list-building strategist (plan traffic and list growth). It ends with a handoff block for a copy or email skill. Body in English, description bilingual.

Many people want an email list but give away things nobody uses (a long ebook, a generic newsletter). The skill picks one urgent problem, three concepts to compare, a format that gives a first result fast, and a follow-up path, all within clear ethical rules (honest scarcity, no tricks). It scores ideas on a four-point checklist, not a vendor scoring system. Reference points: first result in about 15 minutes or less, ideally 5; checklist of 4 points times 5 = 20 (17 to 20 build, 13 to 16 fix the weak point, 12 or less change the format); 3 concepts; validate with 10 to 20 people; a quiz of 3 to 5 situations and 5 to 10 questions; a follow-up of 5 to 6 emails. Every number in this skill is a labelled reference point, not a fact about your market or channel; the skill asks for your own figures and does not use Vietnamese market data.

## When to reach for it

Someone asks for a lead magnet idea, what to give away, an opt-in, a freebie, how to grow an email list, a quiz funnel, a content upgrade or an email course, or says their lead magnet is not converting. For the page and email copy use `copywriter-skill` and `email-specialist`; for the whole-journey view use `journey-mapper`.

## Common questions

**What are the ethical rules?** Honest scarcity, no hidden catches, and a real result for the reader; they are listed in the skill as non-negotiable.

**Why three concepts?** So the choice is a comparison, not a guess.

**Does it write the final copy?** It writes a brief and the asset outline and hands off the copy through a plain-text block.

## It's working if

- The lead magnet solves one named, urgent problem and gives a first result fast.
- Three concepts were compared and scored on the four-point checklist.
- The funnel has a follow-up path and a handoff block.
