# paid-ads-strategist

## What it does

Plans paid advertising on any platform: campaign architecture (objectives, audiences, structure, test design), ad creative drafts (hooks, angles, formats, copy variants), media budget and scaling rules, and cut-hold-scale decision rules. It plans and drafts only: it never creates campaigns, connects ad accounts or spends money. Body in English, description bilingual.

Paid ads usually fail in three ways: no idea what a customer is worth so the budget is arbitrary, tests with too little data to read, and no cut, hold or scale rules set in advance. A cost gate comes first and the skill reads `brand-profile.md` and the do-not-claim list. Platform names appear only as groups (search, social). Worked examples use an invented brand and assumed price and margin figures, labelled. Reference points: budget split 70/20/10; customer value to acquisition cost about 3 to 1; at least 30 to 50 results per variant (10 to 20 on small budgets, with low confidence stated); tests of at least 7 days, no decision under 3 days; cut early at about 3 times the target cost with 0 results; raise budget 10 to 20 percent every 3 to 5 days and stop if cost per result then rises more than about 15 to 20 percent; frequency fatigue above about 3 (cold) or 6 (warm); awareness about 10 to 20 percent of budget; daily budget under about 5 times target cost risks under-delivery. Every number in this skill is a labelled reference point, not a fact about your market or channel; the skill asks for your own figures and does not use Vietnamese market data.

## When to reach for it

Someone asks to plan ads, structure a campaign, write ad copy, find hooks, set an ad budget, or decide what to cut or scale. Money is never spent without the owner's say-so. For the landing page after the click use `cro-specialist`; for deciding a niche use `niche-finder`; for writing at length use `copywriter-skill`.

## Common questions

**Will it launch my campaign?** No. It never creates campaigns, connects accounts or spends money; it writes the plan and drafts.

**Are the budget numbers real?** They are reference points built on assumed price and margin. Replace them with your own.

**What is the cost gate?** A first check that you know what a customer is worth and that spending money has been approved by you.

## It's working if

- The plan starts with the cost gate and states what a customer is worth.
- Each test has a minimum result count, a duration, and a cut-hold-scale rule set before launch.
- The output lists which claims the brand profile forbids, and none are used.
