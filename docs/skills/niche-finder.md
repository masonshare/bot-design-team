# niche-finder

## What it does

Picks one niche to commit to from a broad market map: it scores 3 to 5 candidate niches on five criteria (1 to 5 each, 25 in total) using the user's existing strategy, market, customer-journey and brand material, then recommends one with risks and a handoff to planning. The skill body is in English; the description is bilingual.

After research there is usually a flat list of opportunities, and a plan without one chosen niche stays thin. The skill is pure synthesis: it does no new research and stops if any of its four required inputs is missing, because scoring without evidence is meaningless. Reference points: 20 to 25 is strong (recommend), 15 to 19 is viable (watch), under 15 is dropped. Every number in this skill is a labelled reference point, not a fact about your market; the skill asks for your own figures. Trade-off: it refuses to run without inputs, which costs speed but buys reliability.

## When to reach for it

The user has many options and must choose one before planning: "which niche", "narrow our focus", "pick a market", "scored shortlist". If the market has not been researched yet, do that first, with a market research skill if you have one.

## Common questions

**What inputs does it need?** Four: existing strategy, market, customer-journey and brand material. It stops and says which is missing.

**Can I get a fast version?** A guided mode asks three questions; it is lower confidence and says so.

**Does it research the market?** No. It only reads what you already have.

## It's working if

- It lists missing inputs and stops instead of guessing.
- Each candidate niche has five scores with a one-line evidence note.
- It recommends exactly one niche, with risks and open questions.
