# elite-negotiator

## What it does

A deal strategist in three modes: interrogator (extract the negotiation variables and intelligence), architect (build the tactical strategy, opening, concession plan and walk-away) and coach (live guidance and closing with exact wording). The core artefact is a prep sheet. It scores readiness 0 to 10 and always gives concrete lines to say. Advice only, not legal advice. Body in English, description bilingual.

People often enter a negotiation (salary, a supplier contract, a partnership) without a walk-away limit, an estimate of the other side's alternatives, or wording ready. The earlier 55 frameworks were condensed to 30 with descriptive names, in three reference files and one scenario playbook template; examples are invented. Reference points: readiness is five factors at 2 points each; 8 to 10 moves to architect, 5 to 7 asks for more, under 5 stops to gather facts; 5 to 7 scenarios; a counteroffer ladder of about 65, 85, 95 and 100 percent of target, a starting point to check against your market. Ethics limits apply in every mode. Every number in this skill is a labelled reference point, not a fact about your market; the skill asks for your own figures.

## When to reach for it

Someone asks for help negotiating, a counteroffer, what to ask for, how to respond to an offer, BATNA, a salary, a vendor contract, partnership terms or deal pricing. It does not give legal advice. For presentations to a board use `pitch-bod-bos-dt`.

## Common questions

**Does it give me legal advice?** No. It is strategy and wording only.

**What if I do not know the other side?** Readiness drops; under 5 it stops and asks for what is missing instead of building a strategy on guesses.

**Will it tell me to bluff?** No. The ethics boundaries apply in every mode.

## It's working if

- A prep sheet exists with a walk-away limit, the other side's alternative and the priorities.
- The readiness score decides whether it goes on to strategy or asks more.
- The answer contains exact lines to say, not only principles.
