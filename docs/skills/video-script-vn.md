# video-script-vn

## What it does

Writes Vietnamese video script drafts in three modes: SHORTS (up to about 60 seconds), LONG (long-form, with an optional fact-check variant) and REMAKE (study a reference video's structure, then generate a brand-new idea in the same niche and persona with the same viral structure, never copying its wording or story). The output is a shot list with voice-over, on-screen text and a call to action, plus alternative hooks and a posting pack. Drafts only. Body in Vietnamese, description bilingual.

Channel owners need Vietnamese scripts quickly, with a good hook and the right audience, without repeating themselves or inventing facts. The skill is the execution partner of `tui-kich-ban`, which holds the base knowledge, and it merges three earlier skills into one with three modes. It ends with a list of what the user must fill in or verify. Known weak spot from testing: one run relied on an unchecked technical premise about candles, so verify facts with two independent sources. Reference points: SHORTS up to about 60 seconds; reading speed 150 to 170 words a minute to convert time to word count; about 10 ideas generated for SHORTS and 10 to 20 for LONG; a jolt point every 1 to 2 minutes in LONG; a hook of a few seconds for SHORTS and 30 to 60 seconds for LONG; fact-check variants need at least 2 independent sources. Every number in this skill is a labelled reference point, not a fact about your market or channel; the skill asks for your own figures.

Source: [`packs/marketing-ecommerce/skills/video-script-vn/SKILL.md`](../../packs/marketing-ecommerce/skills/video-script-vn/SKILL.md)

## When to reach for it

Someone asks to write a TikTok or short-video script, a long-form script, or to remake the structure of a reference video for their own idea ("làm lại theo cấu trúc video này"). For the theory behind hooks and formulas use `tui-kich-ban`. It reads `brand-profile.md` first.

## Common questions

**Will REMAKE copy the other video?** No. It studies the structure and generates a new idea and wording for your niche.

**Does it post or publish?** No. It writes drafts only.

**Who checks the facts?** It lists what you must fill in or verify; claims need 2 independent sources.

## It's working if

- The script is a shot list with voice-over, on-screen text and a call to action.
- A hook framework is applied and alternative hooks are offered.
- Facts to verify and blanks to fill are listed at the end, and nothing is copied from the reference video.
