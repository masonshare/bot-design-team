# tui-kich-ban

## What it does

A foundation library for writing Vietnamese video scripts: a seven-point script formula, finding audience pain points, an idea matrix, storytelling structure, hooks, finding questions, and a methodology overview. It answers theory questions and never writes the script itself; writing is handed to `video-script-vn`. Body in Vietnamese, description bilingual. Four reference files hold the detail; the core knowledge is also inside SKILL.md.

Script writers need a quick lookup of the base frameworks without a machine writing for them. The skill keeps knowledge, and the execution lives in `video-script-vn`. All the content was rewritten in new words; none of the earlier sentences, tables, examples or prompts remain, and specific research figures that were quoted in the source were dropped. A reply to a lookup follows a fixed shape and rules for wording apply to anything created from the library. Reference points: a jolt point every 1 to 2 minutes in the proof section; aim for 20 or more titles from one topic with the six-axis matrix; about 4 parts emotion to 6 parts knowledge in story videos; 10 rough questions cut to 1 to 2 for the seven-point question; about 2 hours a month planning the matrix. Known weak spots from testing: in one run it assumed a home-goods niche on its own and gave one claim without grounds; check claims about audiences against your own channel data. Every number in this skill is a labelled reference point, not a fact about your market or channel; the skill asks for your own figures.

Source: [`packs/marketing-ecommerce/skills/tui-kich-ban/SKILL.md`](../../packs/marketing-ecommerce/skills/tui-kich-ban/SKILL.md)

## When to reach for it

Someone asks what a hook is, how the seven-point formula works, how to use an idea matrix, how to find audience pain points, or how storytelling works for video. To actually write a script use `video-script-vn`. It reads `brand-profile.md` if you have one.

## Common questions

**Will it write my script?** No. It explains the framework and hands the writing to `video-script-vn`.

**Does it work for other languages?** It is written for Vietnamese video scripts; the wording rules are Vietnamese.

**Where do the claims about viewers come from?** They are reference points from the method, not measured data for your channel. Check them against your retention graph.

## It's working if

- A lookup answer names the framework, explains it and shows how to apply it, without writing the full script.
- Wording rules for created content are followed.
- Execution requests are handed off to `video-script-vn`.
