# social-content-specialist

## What it does

Plans, drafts and tunes social content across platforms: content pillars, posting calendar, hooks and engagement mechanics, repurposing one idea into many formats, platform-by-platform adaptation, community management basics and measurement. It drafts only and never posts or schedules. Body in English, description bilingual.

Social content is often posted ad hoc, with no pillars, an unsustainable rhythm and weak openings. The skill puts planning, drafting, repurposing, diagnosis, community basics and measurement in one process, and always shows one worked example of repurposing (one idea into at least 3 formats). Platform algorithm notes are short and labelled to verify before relying on them. Reference points: value to ask ratio of 3 to 1 or higher (higher for a new account); 3 to 5 content pillars; up to 8 to 12 sub-topics and 6 to 8 presentation styles; 6 to 12 moments pulled from a source; pre-post self-score of at least 3 out of 5 per variable; at least 3 of 6 share factors per week; repost a winning post after a few months. Every number in this skill is a labelled reference point, not a fact about your market or channel; the skill asks for your own figures and does not use Vietnamese market data.

## When to reach for it

Someone asks for a content calendar, what to post, a LinkedIn post, caption, short-video script, thread or carousel, to repurpose one piece into many, or why a post underperformed. It never publishes or schedules; you post. For long-form search content use `content-strategist-seo`; for sales copy use `copywriter-skill`.

## Common questions

**Will it post for me?** No. It writes drafts and calendars; posting and scheduling stay with you.

**Which platforms does it cover?** It adapts by platform type and labels its platform notes as reference points to verify, since platforms change.

**What if I have one source (a video or an article)?** It pulls 6 to 12 moments from it and turns them into several formats.

## It's working if

- The plan has 3 to 5 pillars and a calendar you can actually sustain.
- Repurposing shows one idea in at least 3 formats.
- Drafts are scored before delivery and nothing is posted or scheduled.
