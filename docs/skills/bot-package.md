# bot-package

## What it does

Packages a designed bot or team for rollout in Orca as numbered manual setup steps or an Orca bot share link, with rollout order and a 10-line summary.

Source: [`packs/starter/dried-plum/skills/bot-package/SKILL.md`](../../packs/starter/dried-plum/skills/bot-package/SKILL.md)

## When to reach for it

You want to export, share or publish a bot or team, or set one up by hand.

## Common questions

**What does a share link carry?** Soul, ticked skills and memory, tool URLs without secrets. See docs/orca-skill-loading.md.

**Is real data included?** Never. Use fake examples only.

**Which comes first?** Score the bot, then package.

## It's working if

- Another person can follow the numbered steps without asking you.
- The share review shows no secret or real data.
- The 10-line summary matches what was packaged.
