# ecommerce-product-strategy

## What it does

Plans the product range for online selling: diagnoses why a product does not sell (shown, clicked, bought), gives each SKU one of three roles (traffic, hero, profit), designs 1 + X bundles, and runs a four-stage test-and-scale loop. It always finishes with a SKU portfolio map.

Owners often treat every product alike and discount the best seller to pull traffic, which thins the margin. The skill diagnoses the funnel before suggesting a product change and forces a continue-or-stop decision after 30 days. Reference points: gross margin about 15-25 percent for traffic SKUs, 30-45 percent for hero SKUs, from 50 percent for profit SKUs; raise ad spend by 20 to 50 percent a week only while profitable. Every number in this skill is a labelled reference point, not a fact about your market; the skill asks for your own figures.

## When to reach for it

An owner asks which product to sell first, how to choose a hero product, how to build a combo, or how to test a new product before buying a lot of stock. For range numbers use `ecommerce-kpi-analytics`; for promotions use `ecommerce-promotion-design`.

## Common questions

**Why diagnose before changing the product?** Many slow sellers have a listing or price problem, not a product problem, and the fix is cheaper.

**Does it pick my products?** It assigns roles to the SKUs you give it and proposes tests; the choice stays yours.

**What is the output?** A portfolio map: each SKU, its role, margin target and next step.

## It's working if

- It diagnoses the funnel before recommending a product change.
- Each SKU ends up with one clear role.
- Test advice includes a stop-or-continue decision at about 30 days.
