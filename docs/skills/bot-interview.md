# bot-interview

## What it does

Interviews you about a bot idea in one batch of questions and gives a go or no-go verdict on whether a bot is the right tool at all. It produces a short brief.

Source: [`skills/design/bot-interview/SKILL.md`](../../skills/design/bot-interview/SKILL.md)

## When to reach for it

You describe a task and ask whether it needs a bot, a skill, a prompt or plain automation, or you must gather requirements or inventory an existing bot.

## Common questions

**How many questions will it ask?** One batch, so you answer once.

**Can the verdict be no?** Yes. A plain automation, prompt or skill is a valid answer.

**What is the brief?** Job, anti-job, user, inputs, outputs, frequency, risks, budget and platform.

## It's working if

- The brief states the job and the anti-job in one line each.
- The verdict names the tool chosen and the reason.
- Nothing was built before the verdict.
