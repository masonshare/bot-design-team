# Scenario playbook template

Use in Architect mode, pick 5 to 7 scenarios likely for this counterparty. Keep it private: it contains your limits and tactics. Not legal advice. All example lines below are invented; replace with the user's real numbers.

## Header

- Deal: [one line] | Type: [salary / vendor / partnership / contract / sale / settlement]
- Target: [number or term] | Reservation: [number or term] | Alternative: [what and how strong]
- Their likely interests: [list, marked assumed or verified]
- Readiness: [0-10] | Overall confidence: [High / Medium / Speculative]

## Common scenarios to choose from

| Group | Scenario | Starter wording (invented) |
|---|---|---|
| Price | Lowball opening | "That is well below what the work is worth. How did you arrive at that figure?" |
| Price | "Your price is too high" | "Compared to what? Let me show what is included." |
| Price | "A competitor is cheaper" | "What does their offer cover on delivery and support? Then we compare like for like." |
| Power | Ultimatum | "I hear that this is firm. What is behind the deadline?" |
| Power | "I have no authority" | "Who decides? Let me prepare a note that helps you make the case." |
| Power | Good cop, bad cop | "I appreciate the flexibility; I will answer the constraints stated by your colleague." |
| Delay | "We need more time" | "Of course. What would you need to see to decide by [date]?" |
| Delay | Silence or ghosting | "Has something changed on your side? A short no is also helpful." |
| Emotion | Anger | "It seems this has been frustrating. Let me make sure I understand what happened." |
| Emotion | Guilt appeal | "I care about the relationship, and I also need the numbers to work for me." |
| Information | "What is your bottom line?" | "I would rather talk about what would make this work for both of us." |
| Closing | Last-minute extras | "I can add that if we close the remaining points as agreed; what are you offering in return?" |
| Closing | "I need to sleep on it" | "Sure. What questions will you take to sleep on, so I can answer them now?" |
| Closing | Walk-away moment | "I think we have reached the limit of what I can do; I would be glad to reopen if circumstances change." |
| Low leverage | No alternative | "I would like to understand your priorities and shape a proposal around them." |
| Low leverage | You made a mistake | "I own that; here is how I will fix it. Can we agree on next steps?" |
| Deadlock | Both entrenched | "Let us list what each side needs, apart from the current positions." |
| Job offer | "Is this your top choice?" | "It is a strong option, and I want to make it work. Here is what would let me say yes." |
| Job offer | Disappointing offer | "I am excited about the role; the base is below what I expected. What flexibility do you have?" |

## Closing checklist

- [ ] Every agreed point is restated and confirmed in writing by both sides.
- [ ] Contract wording reviewed by a qualified professional where it matters.
- [ ] Next step, owner and date agreed.
- [ ] Post-deal review offered, if relevant.
