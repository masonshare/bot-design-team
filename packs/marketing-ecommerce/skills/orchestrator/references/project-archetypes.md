# Project archetypes

Starting sequences, not fixed recipes. Cut phases the user already has, add gates where direction may change. Durations are reference points; check them against the team's real capacity. Every phase ends with a draft for the user to approve; nothing is sent, published or paid for without a go-ahead.

## 1. Launch a product or service

Duration reference: 4 to 8 weeks. Needs: a product and a rough idea of the buyer.

1. `market-researcher`: opportunity and competitors. Move on when the market is clear.
2. `stp-marketing-strategy`: target and positioning. Move on when positioning is approved.
3. `brand-architect`: voice. Move on when voice is approved.
4. `content-strategist-seo`: launch content plan.
5. `lead-gen-expert`: pre-launch lead magnet.
6. `email-specialist`: launch email sequence.
7. `copywriter-skill`: landing page, emails, ads copy.
8. `social-content-specialist`: launch posts.
9. `paid-ads-strategist`: only if there is a budget.
10. `cro-specialist`: audit the landing page before traffic arrives.

Gates: after 1, is the market worth entering; after 6, soft launch or big launch; after 8, organic only or add ads. Lean version: skip 5 and 6.

## 2. Grow an online shop

Duration reference: 2 to 6 weeks, then repeat monthly.

1. `ecommerce-kpi-analytics`: read the numbers and find the bottleneck (traffic, conversion or order value).
2. `ecommerce-channel-strategy`: only if the channel mix is the problem.
3. `ecommerce-store-optimization`: fix shop, category, search and product pages.
4. `ecommerce-image-design`: covers and detail images for the weakest listings.
5. `ecommerce-promotion-design`: a promotion with a profit check.
6. `paid-ads-strategist`: supporting ads, only with budget.
7. `ecommerce-kpi-analytics`: measure the result and record lessons.

Gate after 1: pick the one bottleneck to attack first; do not run steps 3 to 6 all at once.

## 3. Build a brand

Duration reference: 2 to 4 weeks.

1. `market-researcher`: competitor and positioning gaps.
2. `stp-marketing-strategy`: target and positioning.
3. `brand-architect`: voice and identity profile.
4. `copywriter-choi-chu`: Vietnamese name and slogan options, if the brand is Vietnamese-facing.
5. `copywriter-skill`: core brand copy (about text, boilerplate).
6. `ecommerce-image-design`: visual direction, if products are sold online.

Gates: after 2, one brand or several; after 3, bold or friendly tone.

## 4. Run a content month

Duration reference: 4 weeks, then repeat.

1. `brand-architect`: confirm the voice (skip if a brand profile exists).
2. `content-strategist-seo`: clusters and briefs for the month.
3. `copywriter-skill`: pillar articles, then supporting pieces.
4. `social-content-specialist`: repurpose into a posting calendar.
5. `lead-gen-expert`: a content upgrade for the best article.
6. `email-specialist`: a welcome sequence for new subscribers.

Gate after 2: which cluster first. Add the video chain (archetype 8) if the user wants video.

## 5. Build a funnel

Duration reference: 2 to 4 weeks.

1. `journey-mapper`: stages and leaks.
2. `lead-gen-expert`: the offer that exchanges contact details.
3. `email-specialist`: nurture sequence.
4. `copywriter-skill`: capture page, emails, sales page.
5. `cro-specialist`: audit and test hypotheses.
6. `paid-ads-strategist`: send traffic in.

Gate after 1: one step or two steps to capture contact details.

## 6. Fix conversion

Duration reference: 1 to 2 weeks.

1. `journey-mapper` (leak finder): where customers drop.
2. `cro-specialist`: diagnosis and ranked fixes.
3. `copywriter-skill`: rewrite weak headlines and calls to action.
4. `cro-specialist`: test plan.

Gate after 1: the problem is content, page or traffic quality; route accordingly.

## 7. Launch a new physical product

Duration reference: about 4 weeks of planning, plus production time.

1. `new-product-development`: idea to concept and specification.
2. `ecommerce-channel-strategy`: where to sell.
3. `ecommerce-product-strategy`: role in the range, combos.
4. `ecommerce-image-design`: image set.
5. `ecommerce-store-optimization`: product pages.
6. `ecommerce-promotion-design`: launch promotion.
7. `ecommerce-kpi-analytics`: KPIs to watch after launch.

Gate after 1: go or no-go on the concept.

## 8. Video content chain

Duration reference: 1 to 2 weeks per batch.

1. `youtube-digest` (words) or `watch` (visuals): extract ideas from reference videos.
2. `tui-kich-ban`: pick the formula or hook theory.
3. `video-script-vn`: write the script.
4. `copywriter-skill`: call to action and description.
5. `social-content-specialist`: distribution plan.

Gate after 3: short, long or remake; unclear niche goes back to `niche-finder`.

## 9. Package operations into a playbook

Duration reference: 1 to 2 weeks.

1. `ecommerce-kpi-analytics`: fix how success is measured.
2. `playbook-documentation`: SOPs, checklists, handoff notes.

## 10. Custom sequence

1. Write the goal in one sentence and list what already exists.
2. List the skills whose outputs the goal needs; drop the rest.
3. Order them by the dependency rules in the main file.
4. Add a deliverable and a "move on when" to each phase.
5. Mark gates where a result could change direction.
6. If a phase has no matching skill, follow the "when no skill fits" rule.

## Quick pick

| Goal | Archetype |
|---|---|
| New offer to market | 1 |
| Sales stuck on a marketplace or shop | 2 |
| No identity yet | 3 |
| Need steady content | 4 |

| Traffic without conversion | 6 |

| Video series | 8 |
| Make the work repeatable | 9 |
