# Ecosystem map

Every skill that ships in the pack, what it is for, what it needs and where it hands off. Names are exact. Use it to settle close calls between two skills.

## Research and direction

| Skill | Use it for | Needs | Hands off to |
|---|---|---|---|
| `market-researcher` | Market size, competitor profiles, SWOT, Five Forces, positioning, comparison-page briefs | A market or product to study | `stp-marketing-strategy`, `niche-finder` |
| `stp-marketing-strategy` | Segmentation, target choice, positioning statement through Jobs To Be Done, rough 4P | Market facts or customer knowledge | `brand-architect`, `paid-ads-strategist` |
| `niche-finder` | Choose one niche from several, scored with evidence | Strategy, market, journey and brand inputs | Ads, content, email, lead generation |
| `journey-mapper` | Customer journey stages, leaks, ranked fixes | Funnel numbers or a description of the funnel | Any specialist it names per leak |

## Video

| Skill | Use it for | Needs | Hands off to |
|---|---|---|---|
| `video-script-vn` | Vietnamese short, long and remake scripts | Topic, audience, voice | `social-content-specialist` |
| `tui-kich-ban` | Script theory and frameworks; answers questions, does not write scripts | A theory question | `video-script-vn` |
| `youtube-digest` | Summary or notes from a video transcript | A public video link with captions | `video-script-vn`, `content-strategist-seo` |
| `watch` | Understand a video through frames and transcript; a third-party skill, kept as is | A video link or file | Any skill that uses the findings |

## Close calls

- `youtube-digest` or `watch`: words only means `youtube-digest`; what appears on screen matters means `watch`.
- `video-script-vn` or `tui-kich-ban`: writing the script means the first; explaining a technique means the second.
- `copywriter-skill` or `copywriter-choi-chu`: general persuasive copy versus short Vietnamese wording and wordplay.
- `cro-specialist` or `ecommerce-store-optimization`: any page or funnel versus marketplace shop pages with a fixed checklist.
- `journey-mapper` or `ecommerce-kpi-analytics`: customer path in general versus store numbers.
- `market-researcher` or `stp-marketing-strategy`: gathering facts versus choosing target and positioning.
