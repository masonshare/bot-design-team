# Fill-in structures

Starting points only. Replace every `[bracket]`; if you lack the fact, leave a visible placeholder. Examples use an invented brand, "Brightloop", a fictional task app.

## 1. Headlines by awareness level

| Level | Pattern | Invented example |
|---|---|---|
| Unaware | Name the symptom, not the product | "Why does Sunday evening feel like a pile of unfinished things?" |
| Problem-aware | Mirror the pain, hint a way out | "You are not bad at planning. Your list has no end." |
| Solution-aware | Point to the difference | "A to-do list that stops itself at five items." |
| Product-aware | Proof or comparison | "Brightloop vs a plain notes app: what changes in week one [STAT: source]" |
| Most aware | Offer, terms, action | "Brightloop Plus: [price] a month, cancel any time. Start today." |

More patterns: how-to ("How to [result] without [common pain]"), number ("[N] ways to [result]" with a real count), question that only the target reader answers yes to, a plain statement of the benefit, a before-and-after.

## 2. Landing page

1. **Hero:** headline (promise) + one line of support + main CTA + a trust cue (real logo, rating `[REVIEW COUNT: source]`).
2. **Pain or situation:** two to four sentences in the reader's words.
3. **Solution in one line, then how it works** in three steps.
4. **Benefits:** three to five, each as outcome, then feature as reason.
5. **Proof:** testimonials `[TESTIMONIAL: name, role, consent]`, numbers `[STAT: source]`, demo or screenshot.
6. **Objections:** a short FAQ answering price, effort, risk, fit.
7. **Offer and risk reversal:** price, what is included, `[GUARANTEE: client terms]`.
8. **Final CTA:** repeat the promise and the step.

## 3. Sales email body

- **Subject:** specific, under about 50 characters (reference point), no fake "Re:".
- **Opening:** one sentence about the reader's situation.
- **Middle:** one idea, one proof point, one benefit.
- **Close:** one action, a link, a plain sign-off from a real sender.
- **P.S.:** optional: restate the deadline only if real.

Subject-line ideas: a number, a question, a named benefit, a curiosity gap that the body really pays off, a deadline (real).

## 4. Product description

- Line 1: who it is for and the main outcome.
- Line 2 to 4: three benefits, each with the feature that causes it.
- Line 5: the detail that removes doubt (size, material, compatibility, care).
- Line 6: risk removal (returns, warranty from the client) + CTA.

## 5. Social post (short)

Hook line / one concrete detail or story beat / the point / one question or step. No more than one idea.

## 6. Urgency, proof and risk blocks

**Urgency (only if real):** "[Reason the limit exists]. [What ends] on [date]. After that, [what changes]."
**Social proof:** "[N real customers] [did X]" with a source note; quote blocks only with consent: `[TESTIMONIAL: placeholder]`.
**Risk reversal:** "Try it for [period]. If [condition], we [remedy]. [GUARANTEE: confirm terms with client]."

## 7. Story (bridge) outline

Where I was (the situation and the feeling) / what I tried that failed / the moment I saw it differently / what I changed / what happened `[RESULT: real]` / what that means for you.

## 8. CTA bank

Generic to specific: "Submit" to "Send me the guide"; "Buy now" to "Get yours"; "Learn more" to "See how it works"; "Sign up" to "Start my free week".
