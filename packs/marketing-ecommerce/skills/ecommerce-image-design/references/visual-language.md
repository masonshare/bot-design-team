# Visual language cues

Choose only the cues that fit the product, platform and image role. Do not stack every option into one prompt. Write the chosen cues in the prompt language (Vietnamese first, English version after).

## 1. Photo styles

| Style | Cues |
|---|---|
| Clean studio | Seamless white or soft gradient, even soft light, gentle shadow, catalogue look |
| Lifestyle | Believable everyday home or office, warm human moment, product visible but not staged |
| Macro | Extreme close-up, crisp texture, shallow depth, appetising detail |
| Flat lay | Overhead 90 degrees, balanced spacing, natural materials (wood, linen, ceramic, kraft paper) |
| Premium low-key | Dark matte background, controlled highlights, deep soft shadows; for luxury or executive gifts |
| Organic natural | Beige, kraft brown, muted green, off-white; window light; fresh, not clinical |
| Seasonal | Festive palette and motifs for the occasion; refined, one accent colour, clear copy space |

## 2. Angles

| Angle | Effect | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Front | Direct, trustworthy | Cover, label visibility |
| 45 degrees | Volume, depth | Jars, boxes, packed food, gift sets |
| Slightly raised (about 15 degrees) | Clean, commercial | Marketplace hero |
| Eye level | Familiar, human | Lifestyle, usage, gifting scenes |
| Low angle | Powerful, premium | Campaign hero |
| High angle | Friendly, overview | Table scenes, serving |
| Overhead | Organised, contextual | Flat lay, combos, ingredient-led layouts |
| Macro | Tactile quality proof | Texture of fruit, nuts, leaves, spices |

## 3. Composition patterns

- Centred hero: product about 70 to 80 percent of the frame, minimal background (cover, thumbnail). Reference point.
- Product right, copy left (or the mirror): banners and landing heroes; pick the side the package naturally faces.
- Rule of thirds: lifestyle; product on a third line, action supports the story.
- Flat lay grid: ingredients and combos with clear spacing.
- Open-box reveal: gift sets; box open, arrangement, card, ribbon, clean zone for a client logo.
- Foreground, midground, background: premium scenes; props softly blurred behind.

## 4. Lighting

| Light | Effect | Phrase to use |
|---|---|---|
| Soft studio | Clean, safe | soft diffused studio light from upper left |
| Window light | Warm, trustworthy | warm natural window light from the left, soft shadows |
| Golden hour | Emotional, festive | warm low sun, gentle highlights |
| Low-key | Luxury | low-key light, controlled highlights, deep soft shadows |
| Bright even | Clarity, specs | bright even light, no harsh shadow |
| Rim light | Separation | subtle back rim light separating product from background |

## 5. Food, beverage and gift-set cues

- Food and drink: show freshness and texture; use the real ingredients of the product only; no exaggerated health or medical look.
- Materials for props: wood, ceramic, linen, kraft paper, bamboo tray, dried leaves.
- Gift props: box, sleeve or band, ribbon, greeting card, logo placeholder, thank-you card.
- Occasions: choose the palette and motif from the client's own market and calendar; keep one accent, not a collage.
- Business gifting: clean premium table, office or desk moment, bulk arrangement, a card with a clear logo zone.
- Avoid settings foreign to the target market unless requested, and avoid fake certification or award visuals.

## 6. Generate or place manually

Ask the generator for: background, scene, props, lighting, mood, angle, short quoted copy with its layout, simple infographic layouts built from verified copy.

Place from real files in editing software: logos, QR codes, SKU marks, certificates, real package labels, legal text, client-specific logos, licensed typefaces that a generator cannot reproduce.

When exact product or box fidelity is mandatory, generate the background only and composite the real product cutout.
