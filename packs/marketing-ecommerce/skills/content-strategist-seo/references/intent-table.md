# Intent table and results-page check

All thresholds here are reference points. Check them against the live results page and the user's own data.

## Four base intents

| Intent | Typical query shape | What the results page shows | Best format | Call to action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Informational | what, why, how, guide, examples | guides, definitions, videos, answer boxes, "people also ask" | explainer, how-to, glossary entry | soft: subscribe, read next |
| Navigational | brand or product name, "login" | the brand's own pages, site links | do not target unless it is your brand | none |
| Commercial investigation | best, vs, review, alternatives, for [use case] | comparison lists, reviews, forum threads | comparison, ranked list, review with a stated method | medium: demo, trial, shortlist |
| Transactional | buy, price, discount, near me, order | product and category pages, shopping blocks, local packs | product or landing page, pricing page | hard: buy, book, start |

## Extra lenses when the four are not enough

- Local: map results appear; needs location data and a business profile.
- News or freshness: recent dates dominate; needs a fast, dated page.
- Visual or video: image or video blocks lead; needs original media.
- Task or tool: calculators and converters rank; needs a working tool.
- Problem-solving: forum and troubleshooting threads rank; needs a tested fix.

## Results-page check (about five minutes)

1. Open the top 10. Count each content type: guide, list, product, tool, video, forum.
2. Note the format the top 3 share (steps, table, long guide, short answer).
3. Note the angle: beginner, expert, budget, fast, specific audience.
4. Note answer boxes, video blocks, shopping blocks and local packs; each can reduce clicks on plain pages.
5. Decide: match type and format, then differentiate on depth, proof, data or clarity.

## Mixed intent scoring

Give each intent a score of 0 to 3 by how many results of that type appear.
- One intent at 3 and the rest 0 to 1: single intent, match it.
- Two intents at 2 or more: split; pick the segment that fits the business.
- Three or more at 2 or more: too broad; target a more specific long-tail query.
- Nothing above 1: ambiguous; add a modifier or drop it.

Evolving intent: the same person moves from learning, to comparing, to buying. Cover each step with its own page and link them in that order.

## Intent shifts

When an algorithm update or a change in user behaviour makes the results page switch type, the old page can fall without being "worse". Recheck the results page for your top queries each quarter; if the winning type changed, rebuild to the new type or accept the loss.
