### 8. SEO notes

### 9. Anti-pattern check (tick before delivery)
- [ ] No attack on the competitor, every claim sourced and dated
- [ ] No unproved superlatives
- [ ] The table does not show us winning every row
- [ ] No outdated price or feature
- [ ] Helps a buyer decide, not a rewrite of the product page
- [ ] No trademark misuse or implied endorsement

Finish with the handoff block from the skill (copywriting for the draft, SEO content for the keyword plan).
